# Einride Partners With Texas Highway Operator to Create Autonomous Freight Testbed

- **Published:** `2026-03-17T11:30:00Z`
- **Language:** `en`

## Original Cision Formatted Body

<p><span><span><span><span><strong><span><span>STOCKHOLM, SWEDEN &amp; AUSTIN, TX </span></span></strong></span></span></span><span><span><span><span><span><span>&ndash; Einride (&quot;Einride&quot; or the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;), a technology company driving the transition to cost-efficient electric and autonomous freight operations for some of the world&#39;s largest shippers, today announced it has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with SH 130 Concession Company to position SH 130, which connects Austin and San Antonio, as the preferred corridor for autonomous freight operations in Texas.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Einride is the first autonomous truck company to partner with SH 130 Concession Company in this initiative. The collaboration will establish an infrastructure-ready testbed for Einride&rsquo;s purpose built, cab-less autonomous freight vehicles. It will support the safety validation of autonomous highway operations on Segments 5 and 6 of SH 130, including integration with adjacent frontage roads to enable seamless first- and last-mile connectivity and to optimize vehicle performance and energy efficiency.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>&quot;This partnership with SH 130 Concession Company marks an important step forward in proving the scalability and economic benefits of electric and autonomous freight,&rdquo; said Roozbeh Charli, Chief Executive Officer of Einride. &ldquo;By establishing this testbed, Einride is further cementing Austin, Texas as a hub for our American autonomous freight operations, collaborating with infrastructure operators and regulators to scale safe, performance-based deployments.&rdquo;</span></span></span></span></span></span><br>
<br>
<span><span><span><span><span><span><span>With its Freight-Capacity-as-a-Service business model, Einride combines autonomous and electric trucks, AI optimization software, and charging infrastructure into one integrated solution that delivers reliability and scale. </span></span></span></span></span></span></span><span><span><span><span><span><span>The Company operates one of the world&rsquo;s largest electric heavy-duty fleets, serving customers across the U.S., Europe and the Middle East, with autonomous trucks in daily operations with customers in both the U.S and in Europe. Einride&rsquo;s safety framework has undergone independent audit and aligns with internationally recognized safety and cybersecurity standards.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>SH 130 Concession Company manages and operates the 41-mile southern section of the SH 130 toll road that serves as a reliable alternative to I-35, the main U.S. freight artery connecting Mexico to Canada. Providing a strategic route for fleets to move freight reliably through the fast-growing Central Texas region, the southern section of SH 130 continues to offer ample capacity to accommodate future traffic growth. For Einride, the partnership represents a natural expansion opportunity.&nbsp;</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Einride and SH 130 Concession Company also plan to draft a blueprint for a next-generation rest stop designed to manage electric autonomous trucks, incorporating high-capacity EV charging and specialized docking requirements. The two companies will also investigate the technical feasibility of integrating Einride&rsquo;s AI-powered optimization software, Saga AI, with SH 130 Concession Company&rsquo;s digital ecosystem to enhance data sharing, traffic management, and operational efficiency.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>&ldquo;We&rsquo;re proud to partner with Einride to advance our goal to make SH 130 the premier smart freight corridor in the state,&rdquo; said SH 130 Concession Company CEO, Ananth Prasad. &ldquo;We&rsquo;re actively working with freight customers, industry partners and TxDOT to implement new technology solutions and by a</span></span></span></span></span></span></span><span><span><span><span><span><span>ligning digital infrastructure, connectivity readiness, and future charging capacity, SH 130 Concession Company is advancing its ambition to establish the roadway as a nationally recognized route for autonomous freight movement</span></span></span></span></span></span><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>.&rdquo;</span></span></span></span></span></span></span><br>
<br>
<span><span><span><span><span><span>In late 2025, Einride announced plans to pursue a public listing on the New York Stock Exchange through a proposed business combination with Legato Merger Corp. III (NYSEAMERICAN: LEGT), a special purpose acquisition company. The Company has established strong commercial traction, with more than 25 enterprise customers across seven countries, approximately $65 million in expected annual recurring revenue (ARR) from signed customer contracts, and over $800 million in potential long-term ARR through joint business plans with blue-chip customers.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>
