# Einride Publishes Voluntary Safety Self-Assessment for Autonomous Heavy-Duty Trucks

- **EncryptedId:** `33A37EB302180B6D`
- **Published:** `2026-03-25T11:30:00Z`
- **Language:** `en`
- **Source:** <https://news.cision.com/einride/r/einride-publishes-voluntary-safety-self-assessment-for-autonomous-heavy-duty-trucks,c4326236>

## Original Cision Formatted Body

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>By publishing its VSSA, Einride reinforces its commitment to transparency, regulatory collaboration, and the responsible scaling of autonomous freight technology. The Company continues to engage proactively with the U.S. Department of Transportation, </span></span></span></span></span></span><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)</span></span></span></span></span></span></span><span><span><span><span><span><span>, and international authorities as it expands commercial autonomous operations.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Einride&rsquo;s VSSA details the safety framework supporting its proprietary autonomous technology platform, which integrates its AI-powered optimization software, Saga AI, with the Einride Driver, a vehicle agnostic autonomous drive software developed in-house to enable safe, driverless freight operations. Together, these systems support Einride&rsquo;s Freight-Capacity-as-a-Service (FCaaS) and Technology Licensing offerings.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>At the center of the VSSA is Einride&rsquo;s cab-less, cargo-only, electric autonomous truck, purpose-built from the ground up for driverless operation. Einride&rsquo;s vehicle features integrated redundancy across steering, braking, power, sensing, and compute systems, enabling fail-safe and fail-operational performance.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Einride&rsquo;s approach to autonomy is grounded in a rigorous, documented safety case, audited by third parties and aligned with recognized standards including UL 4600, ISO 26262, and ISO/PAS 21448. The safety case defines the system&rsquo;s Operational Design Domain (ODD), performance requirements, fallback strategies, and lifecycle safety obligations.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>The VSSA outlines how the Company:</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<ul>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Uses a combination of machine-learning&ndash;based driving with an independent, deterministic safety checker.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Employs a diverse and redundant sensor suite (camera, radar, lidar) and high-definition mapping.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Implements structured minimal risk maneuvers and automatic safe-state transitions when system or environmental limits are reached.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Verifies performance through simulation, hardware- and vehicle-in-the-loop testing, proving-ground validation, and site acceptance testing prior to deployment.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span><br>
	&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>The VSSA describes Einride&rsquo;s Safety Management System (SMS), informed by best practices from aviation and defense. The SMS governs risk management, safety assurance, continuous monitoring, and a fleet-wide grounding policy, and is overseen by an independent Safety &amp; Security Management function.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Additional topics covered include:</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<ul>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Vehicle cybersecurity and data protection, aligned with </span></span></span></span></span></span><span><span><span><strong><span><span>ISO 21434</span></span></strong></span></span></span><span><span><span><span><span><span> and </span></span></span></span></span></span><span><span><span><strong><span><span>ISO 27001</span></span></strong></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Crashworthiness and post-crash behavior</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Event data recording and privacy compliance</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Emergency response planning and first-responder training</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span><span><span>Ongoing engagement with regulators at the federal, state, and local levels</span></span></span></span></span></span></span><br>
	&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
